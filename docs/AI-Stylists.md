# ✨ The Dressing Room

# AI Stylist System

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Overview

The Dressing Room should not have a single generic AI assistant.

Instead, users should experience a personalized AI Glam Squad made up of different specialists with unique personalities, expertise, and styling philosophies.

The purpose of the AI Stylist System is to make users feel like they have access to:

* a personal stylist
* a fashion editor
* a beauty consultant
* a shopping concierge
* a supportive best friend

The AI team should feel warm, intelligent, culturally aware, and deeply personalized.

---

# 2. Core AI Philosophy

The AI stylists should never make users feel judged.

The goal is not:

"Your outfit is wrong."

The goal is:

"Let's make this look even more you."

The AI should:

* celebrate personal style
* encourage experimentation
* respect individuality
* consider lifestyle realities
* understand cultural contexts
* provide constructive recommendations

The user should always feel more confident after interacting with the AI.

---

# 3. AI Memory System

The AI stylist team should learn from the user's fashion journey.

The AI should remember:

## Style Preferences

* disliked colors
* favorite silhouettes
* preferred fits
* favorite fabrics
* preferred aesthetics

## Shopping Behavior

* favorite brands
* frequently purchased categories
* impulse purchases

* investment pieces

## Wardrobe History

* most worn items
* favorite outfits

* rarely worn pieces
* seasonal patterns

## Lifestyle

* work environment
* travel habits
* event types
* social activities
* comfort preferences

## Beauty Profile

* hairstyles
* makeup preferences
* jewelry preferences
* fragrance preferences

The AI should become more personalized over time.

# 10. AI Collaboration

The stylists should work together.

"Let's make an unforgettable entrance."
