# ✨ The Dressing Room

## AI Luxury Image Consultant & Digital Wardrobe Platform

## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Product Overview

## Vision

The Dressing Room is a luxury AI-powered personal styling platform designed to help users discover, organize, and express their personal style.

The application transforms the traditional digital closet into an immersive luxury dressing experience where users can manage their wardrobe, receive AI styling guidance, plan outfits, organize beauty routines, evaluate purchases, and build their personal image.

The experience should feel like stepping into a private couture dressing suite with a personal glam squad available at all times.

This is not a simple wardrobe inventory app.

It is a complete personal style ecosystem combining:

* fashion
* beauty
* lifestyle
* shopping intelligence
* event planning
* travel preparation
* cultural expression
* personal confidence

---

# 2. Mission Statement

Create the ultimate AI-powered digital dressing room that helps users feel seen, confident, and beautifully styled every day.

The platform should combine the convenience of artificial intelligence with the emotional experience of having a trusted personal stylist, beauty consultant, and fashion expert.

---

# 3. Target Audience

## Primary Audience

Black women who love fashion, beauty, luxury, self-expression, and intentional personal style.

The platform is designed with Black women as the center of the experience.

The application should naturally understand and support:

* natural hair
* wigs
* protective styles
* braids
* locs
* sew-ins
* silk presses
* makeup diversity
* melanin-friendly beauty recommendations
* luxury fashion
* professional style
* event dressing
* cultural fashion moments
* Divine Nine organization events

The app welcomes all users, but the product experience should reflect the needs, preferences, and realities of Black women.
