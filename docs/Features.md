# 1. Product Overview

The Dressing Room is an AI-powered luxury digital wardrobe and personal image platform.

The application combines:

* wardrobe management
* AI styling
* beauty intelligence
* shopping assistance
* event planning
* travel styling
* cultural fashion experiences
* personal analytics

The goal is to create the feeling of having a complete personal glam team available anytime.
